Airport

Thiruvananthapuram International Airport (TRV) is the nearest airport to the Munroe Island area. Use the retreat’s Google Maps pin to plan your onward journey.

Local transportation

Kollam Junction and Munroturuttu are rail options for the area. If arriving by boat, call the centre to confirm the best jetty and current timings.

Still waters. Open hearts.

{{ aboutIntroduction }} This hidden gem, located on the shimmering Ashtamudi Lake, offers an atmosphere of unmatched tranquility and lush greenery, framed by towering coconut palms that sway gently in the breeze, carrying the fresh scent of nature over the sparkling waters.

A perfect fusion of natural beauty and divine presence, Dhyanatheeram is an ideal sanctuary for conferences, spiritual retreats, rejuvenating holidays, and peaceful relaxation. The breathtaking vistas of the rising and setting sun, along with the vibrant hues of the landscape, showcase the magnificent artistry of the Creator.

Here, amidst the peaceful embrace of nature, you'll find a unique opportunity to reconnect with the divine and experience the pure serenity that only this sacred place can offer. Come, feel the spirit of nature, and glorify the Almighty God in the heart of God's creation.
